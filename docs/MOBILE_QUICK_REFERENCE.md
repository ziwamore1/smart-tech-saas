# SmartTech Mobile App — Quick Reference

> **Print this guide for staff and parents**

---

## Logging In

1. Open the **SmartTech** app
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Tap **Sign In**

---

## Your Home Screen (Dashboard)

After login, you see your **Dashboard** with:
- **Quick stats** — your key numbers at a glance
- **Recent activity** — latest updates
- **Quick actions** — common tasks

---

## Navigation (Bottom Tabs)

| Icon | Tab | What's There |
|------|-----|-------------|
| 🏠 | Home | Your dashboard |
| 📊 | Results | Exam results, report cards |
| 📅 | Schedule | Weekly timetable |
| ✅ | Attendance | Mark or view attendance |
| 💬 | Messages | Talk to teachers/parents |

**Tap any tab** to switch screens. **Pull down** to refresh.

---

## By Role — What You Can Do

### 👨‍🏫 Teachers
- **Take Attendance:** Tap ✅ → Select class → Mark students Present/Absent/Late → Save
- **Enter Marks:** Tap 📊 → Select exam → Enter marks per student → Save
- **View Timetable:** Tap 📅 → See your weekly schedule
- **Message Parents:** Tap 💬 → New Message → Select parent → Type message → Send

### 🏫 Class Teachers
Everything a Teacher can do, plus:
- **Manage Class:** See all your class students
- **View Class Analytics:** See attendance rates, performance trends
- **Generate Reports:** Create class summaries

### 👑 Directors / Head Teachers
Everything above, plus:
- **Manage Staff:** Add teachers, assign roles
- **Manage Classes:** Create classes, assign class teachers
- **Approve Results:** Review and publish exam results
- **Send Announcements:** Message the whole school

### 👨‍🎓 Students
- **View Results:** Tap 📊 → See your grades and report cards
- **Check Timetable:** Tap 📅 → See your class schedule
- **View Attendance:** Tap ✅ → See your attendance record
- **Message Teachers:** Tap 💬 → Ask questions

### 👨‍👩‍👦 Parents
- **View Children:** See all your children in one place
- **Check Results:** Tap 📊 → Select child → See their grades
- **Check Attendance:** Tap ✅ → See if your child is present/absent
- **Message Teachers:** Tap 💬 → Contact any teacher
- **Get Alerts:** Receive notifications when your child is absent

## Common Tasks — Step by Step

### Take Attendance (Teacher)
1. Tap the **✅ Attendance** tab
2. Select your **class** and **date**
3. For each student, tap: **Present** (green), **Absent** (red), or **Late** (yellow)
4. Tap **Save**
5. Parents are automatically notified of absences

### Enter Marks (Teacher)
1. Tap the **📊 Results** tab
2. Select the **exam** and **subject**
3. Enter marks in the grid next to each student's name
4. Tap **Save**
5. Results are published after approval

### View My Results (Student)
1. Tap the **📊 Results** tab
2. See all your exam results
3. Tap any exam for detailed grades
4. Tap **Report Card** to download your report

### Check My Child (Parent)
1. Tap the **📊 Results** tab
2. Select your **child's name**
3. See their results, attendance, and report cards
4. Tap any section for details

### Send a Message
1. Tap the **💬 Messages** tab
2. Tap **+ New** (or the compose icon)
3. Select the **recipient** (teacher, parent, or student)
4. Type your message
5. Tap **Send**
