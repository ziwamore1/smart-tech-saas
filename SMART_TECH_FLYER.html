Document Security

Three Layers of Document Authentication

Every issued document is stamped, QR-verified, and cryptographically signed — verifiable by anyone, anywhere.

🔏

Digital Stamps

Custom circular, rectangular, or oval stamps with school emblem, serial number, and verification marker. SVG rendering with ink effects.

📱

QR Verification

Every document gets a unique QR code. Anyone scans it at /v/{code} — sees Valid, Superseded, Expired, or Revoked status. No login needed.

🔐

Cryptographic Signatures

Ed25519 digital signatures with AES-256-GCM encryption. SHA-256 document hashing. Institutional X.509 certificates. PDF embedded verification.
