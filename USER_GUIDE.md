## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Logging In (Web)

1. Open your browser and go to **app.smarttechsaas.com**
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Click **Sign In**
4. You'll be taken to your role-based dashboard

### 1.2 Logging In (Mobile)

1. Open the **SmartTech** app on your phone
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Tap **Sign In**
4. Your dashboard loads based on your role

### 1.3 First-Time Setup (Director Only)

If you're the first Director logging in:

1. You'll be prompted to complete your school profile
2. Set your **school name**, **institution type** (Primary, Secondary, College, University)
3. Add your first staff members via **Users** or **School Members**
4. Set up **Classes** and **Academic Years**

### 1.4 Understanding Your Role

Your role determines what you can see and do:

| Role | Can Do |
|------|--------|
| **Director** | Full school management — users, staff, classes, finances, settings |
| **Head Teacher** | Academic management — teachers, classes, assessments, results |
| **Deputy Head** | Support academic management — attendance, lessons, reports |
| **Lower Primary Senior Teacher** | Oversee Pre-School to Grade 4 — manage attendance, results, assessments |
| **Upper Primary Senior Teacher** | Oversee Grade 5 to Grade 7 — approve assessments, manage ECZ prep |
| **Teacher** | View classes, enter marks, manage attendance for assigned subjects |
| **Class Teacher** | Manage own class — students, attendance, results, communications |
| **Student** | View own results, timetable, attendance, homework |
| **Parent** | View children's results, attendance, communicate with teachers |
| **SuperAdmin** | Platform-wide administration — manage all schools, subscriptions, features |

## 2. Your Dashboard

### 2.1 Dashboard Overview

After login, you see your **Dashboard** with:

- **Quick Stats** — student count, teacher count, attendance rate, etc.
- **Recent Activity** — latest notifications and updates
- **Quick Actions** — common tasks for your role
- **Charts** — enrollment trends, performance analytics

### 2.2 Sidebar Navigation

The left sidebar shows all pages you can access. Click any item to navigate. The sidebar adapts to your role — you only see what's relevant to you.

### 2.3 Notifications

Click the **bell icon** in the top-right to see notifications. You'll be notified about:
- New messages from teachers/parents
- Exam results published
- Attendance alerts
- System updates

## 3. Managing Users & Roles

> **Who can do this:** Director, SuperAdmin

### 3.1 Viewing All Users

1. Click **Users** in the sidebar
2. See a table of all staff with their **name**, **email**, **status**, and **roles**
3. Use the **search bar** to find a specific person
4. Use the **role filter** to show only users with a specific role

### 3.2 Assigning a Role

1. Find the user in the table
2. Click the **+ Role** button in the Actions column
3. Select the role from the dropdown
4. The role is assigned immediately

### 3.3 Changing a Role

1. Hover over the role badge on a user's row
2. Click the **edit icon** (pencil) that appears
3. Select the new role from the modal
4. The old role is removed and the new one is assigned

### 3.4 Role Overview

At the bottom of the Users page, **Role Overview cards** show how many staff members have each role. Click a card to filter the table.

---

## 4. School Membership

> **Who can do this:** Director, SuperAdmin

The **School Members** page manages who belongs to your school and their roles.

### 4.1 Viewing Members

1. Click **School Members** in the sidebar
2. See all members with their **name**, **email**, **status**, and **assigned roles**
3. Use **search** and **role filter** to find specific members

### 4.2 Adding a New Member

1. Click **+ Add Member** in the top-right
2. Search for the user by **name** or **email**
3. Click **+ Add** next to the user you want to add
4. The user is now a member of your school

### 4.3 Assigning a School Role

1. Find the member in the table
2. Click **+ Role** in the Actions column
3. Select the role from the dropdown
4. The role is assigned

### 4.4 Removing a Role

1. Hover over the role badge on a member's row
2. Click the **×** button that appears
3. Confirm the removal

### 4.5 Removing a Member

1. Click the **🗑️** button in the Actions column
2. Confirm the removal
3. This removes the member and all their school roles

## 5. Teachers & Staff

> **Who can do this:** Director, Head Teacher

### 5.1 Staff Register

1. Click **Teachers** in the sidebar
2. See a list of all staff with their **name**, **department**, **staff type**, and **status**
3. Use filters to narrow by **department**, **staff type**, or **status**

### 5.2 Adding a Teacher

1. Click **+ Add Teacher** in the top-right
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Name** (first and last)
   - **Email**
   - **Phone**
   - **Department** (e.g., Mathematics, Sciences, Lower Primary)
   - **Staff Type** (Teaching or Non-Teaching)
   - **Role** (e.g., Teacher, Class Teacher, Head Teacher)
   - **Qualification** and **Specialization**
3. Click **Add Teacher**

### 5.3 Assigning Subjects to Teachers

1. Click **+ Assign Classes** (or **+ Assign Subjects** for secondary schools)
2. Select the **Teacher**
3. Select the **Class**
4. For secondary: select the **Subject**
5. Select the **Academic Year**
6. Click **Create Assignment**

### 5.4 Viewing a Teacher Profile

1. Click on a teacher's **name** or the **view icon**
2. See their full profile: contact info, department, qualifications, assigned classes

### 5.5 Editing a Teacher

1. Click the **✏️ edit icon** on the teacher's row
2. Update any fields
3. Click **Save Changes**

### 5.6 Deleting a Teacher

1. Click the **🗑️ delete icon** on the teacher's row
2. Confirm the deletion

## 6. Classes & Class Teachers

> **Who can do this:** Director, Head Teacher, Upper Primary Senior Teacher

### 6.1 Viewing Classes

1. Click **Classes** in the sidebar
2. See all classes as **cards** showing:
   - Class name and level
   - Assigned class teacher
   - Student count (total, male, female)

### 6.2 Creating a Class

1. Click **+ Add Class**
2. Enter the **class name** (e.g., "Grade 5A", "Form 2B")
3. Select the **level type** (Grade, Form, etc.)
4. Set the **capacity** (max students)
5. Select a **grading system**
6. Click **Create**

### 6.3 Assigning a Class Teacher

1. Click the **🏫 icon** on a class card
2. See any **current assignments**
3. To assign a new class teacher:
   - Select the **Teacher**
   - Select the **Academic Year**
   - Check **"Primary class teacher"** if they're the homeroom teacher
4. Click **Assign**

### 6.4 Removing a Class Teacher

1. Click the **🏫 icon** on the class card
2. Find the assignment you want to remove
3. Click **Remove** and confirm

### 6.5 Adding Subjects to a Class

1. Click the **📚 icon** on a class card
2. Search for a subject to add
3. Click **Add** next to the subject
4. To remove: click **Remove** next to the subject

### 6.6 Viewing Class Students

1. Click the **👥 Students** button on a class card
2. See all enrolled students
3. Enroll new students or remove existing ones

## 7. Students

> **Who can do this:** Director, Head Teacher, Class Teacher, Teacher (view only)

### 7.1 Viewing Students

1. Click **Students** in the sidebar
2. See all students with their **name**, **class**, **status**, and **parent info**
3. Use **search** and **class filter** to find students

### 7.2 Adding a Student

1. Click **+ Add Student**
2. Fill in: name, date of birth, gender, parent/guardian info
3. Select the **class** to enroll them in
4. Click **Add Student**

### 7.3 Student Profile

1. Click on a student's name to see their full profile
2. View: personal info, enrolled classes, attendance history, results

## 8. Assessments & Exams

> **Who can do this:** Director, Head Teacher, Teacher (own subjects), Upper Primary Senior Teacher (approve)

### 8.1 Creating an Exam

1. Go to **Exams** in the sidebar
2. Click **+ Create Exam**
3. Enter: exam name, type (Mid-Term, Final, Quiz), date range
4. Select the **class** and **subjects**
5. Click **Create**

### 8.2 Entering Marks

1. Open the exam
2. Select a **subject** and **class**
3. Enter marks for each student in the grid
4. Click **Save Marks**

### 8.3 Publishing Results

1. After entering all marks, review the **grading summary**
2. Click **Publish** to make results visible to students and parents

### 8.4 Approving Results (Upper Primary Senior Teacher)

1. Go to **Assessment Oversight** in the sidebar
2. See pending assessments awaiting approval
3. Review the marks and grading
4. Click **Approve** or **Request Changes**

## 9. Attendance

> **Who can do this:** Class Teacher, Teacher (assigned classes)

### 9.1 Taking Attendance

1. Open your class or go to **Attendance**
2. Select the **date** and **class**
3. Mark each student as **Present**, **Absent**, or **Late**
4. Click **Save Attendance**

### 9.2 Viewing Attendance History

1. Go to **Attendance** → **History**
2. Select a **date range** and **class**
3. See the attendance report with percentages

### 9.3 Attendance Alerts

- Parents receive notifications when their child is marked absent
- Directors see attendance rates on the dashboard

## 10. Results & Report Cards

> **Who can do this:** Director, Head Teacher, Class Teacher (own class), Student (own results), Parent (children's results)

### 10.1 Viewing Results

**Students:** Go to **Results** to see your exam results and grades.

**Parents:** Go to **Children** → select a child → **Results** to see their performance.

**Teachers:** Go to **Results** to see class results for your subjects.

### 10.2 Generating Report Cards

1. Go to **Report Cards** (Director/Head Teacher)
2. Select the **term** and **class**
3. Click **Generate Report Cards**
4. Download as **PDF** for printing

### 10.3 Grading System

SmartTech uses configurable grading systems:

**Primary School (Zambia):**
- One: 80-100% (A)
- Two: 70-79% (B)
- Three: 60-69% (C)
- Four: 40-49% (D)
- Five: 0-39% (F)

**Secondary School:**
- A: 75-100%
- B: 65-74%
- C: 50-64%
- D: 40-49%
- F: 0-39%

## 11. Timetable

> **Who can do this:** Director, Head Teacher (manage), Teacher/Student (view)

### 11.1 Viewing the Timetable

**Students:** Tap **Timetable** on the mobile app or click in the sidebar on web.

**Teachers:** See your weekly schedule with class times and subjects.

### 11.2 Creating a Timetable

1. Go to **Timetable** → **Manage**
2. Select the **class** and **academic year**
3. Add time slots: **subject**, **teacher**, **day**, **start/end time**
4. Save the timetable

### 12.1 Sending a Message

1. Go to **Communications** in the sidebar
2. Click **+ New Message**
3. Select the **recipient** (teacher, parent, student, or class)
4. Type your message
5. Click **Send**

### 12.2 Viewing Messages

1. Go to **Communications** → **Inbox**
2. See all messages sorted by date
3. Click a message to read it

### 12.3 Announcements

1. Go to **Communications** → **Announcements**
2. Directors can create school-wide announcements
3. Teachers can create class announcements

## 14. Platform Administration (SuperAdmin)

> **Who can do this:** SuperAdmin only

### 14.1 SuperAdmin Dashboard

1. Login with a SuperAdmin account
2. See **platform-wide stats**: total schools, total users, subscriptions

### 14.2 Managing Schools

1. Click **Schools** in the sidebar
2. See all registered schools
3. Click a school to view details, manage subscription, or disable

### 14.3 Platform Role Management

1. Click **Platform Roles** in the sidebar
2. See a sidebar of all platform roles (SuperAdmin, Director, Teacher, etc.)
3. Click a role to see all users with that role
4. **Assign Role:** Click **+ Assign Role**, search for a user, click **+ Assign**
5. **Remove Role:** Click **Remove** next to a user and confirm

### 14.4 Subscription Plans

1. Click **Subscription Plans**
2. Create and manage subscription tiers (Basic, Standard, Premium)
3. Set feature limits per tier

### 14.5 Institution Types

1. Click **Institution Types**
2. Manage what roles and features are available for each institution type (Primary, Secondary, College, University)

## 16. Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Can't login | Check email/password. Contact admin if account is locked. |
| No sidebar items | Your role may not have access. Contact your Director. |
| Can't see students | Check your class assignments in Teachers page. |
| Results not showing | Results may not be published yet. Contact your teacher. |
| Mobile app crashing | Update to the latest version. Clear app cache. |
| Slow loading | Check internet connection. Try refreshing the page. |

### Getting Help

- **In-App Help:** Click the **?** icon on any page
- **Email:** support@smarttechsaas.com
- **Documentation:** docs.smarttechsaas.com
